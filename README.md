Description:

The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requires equal access to information, services, and technology for people with disabilities. This includes digital content such as websites and PDF documents.
Under U.S. Department of Justice requirements, large state and local government agencies must make their digital services accessible by April 26, 2027. Failure to meet accessibility requirements can result in citizen complaints, DOJ investigations, lawsuits, legal settlements, and the continued exclusion of people with disabilities from essential public services.
ADA Accessibility Checker is an AI-powered application that scans and audits PDF documents to determine whether people with disabilities can access and use them. The solution evaluates documents against recognized accessibility standards, including Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) and PDF/UA.
The application identifies common accessibility barriers, generates clear audit findings, and provides actionable remediation guidance. By automating document analysis, government agencies can evaluate large volumes of PDFs more efficiently, prioritize accessibility issues, reduce compliance risks, and create more inclusive digital experiences.

Problem or opportunity statement

State and local government agencies manage large volumes of PDF documents that may contain accessibility barriers for people with disabilities. Manual auditing is slow, costly, and difficult to scale. The opportunity is to use AI to automatically evaluate PDFs against ADA, WCAG, and PDF/UA standards, identify accessibility issues, and provide actionable remediation guidance.
